Terms

Use clubyclub thoughtfully.

clubyclub helps student clubs and students discover potential leadership opportunities. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee a role, an interview, or club placement.

Accurate information

Please submit an email address you control and choose the signup option that best describes what you are looking for. Do not submit someone else’s address or use the form to abuse, scrape, or disrupt the service.

Early-stage service

Features, availability, and matching processes may change as clubyclub develops. We may contact waitlist members about relevant opportunities and early access.

Questions

Contact hello@clubyclub.com with questions about these terms.
