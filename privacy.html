Privacy

We keep signup data focused.

When you join the clubyclub waitlist, we collect the email address, signup choice, selected role when provided, timestamps, and basic browser information needed to operate the signup flow.

How we use it

We use this information to manage the waitlist, send signup confirmations, understand which club opportunities people want, and send the clubyclub team an operational summary.

Where it is stored

Signup records are stored in the clubyclub operations Google Sheet and processed by the connected Google Apps Script. Confirmation emails are sent from clubyclub.ops@gmail.com.

Your choices

To ask about your signup or request removal, email hello@clubyclub.com from the address you used.
